Why the Land Stack is Feasible

The technology is proven. The real challenge is state-by-state integration—which we solve via Config-Driven Adapters.

TECHNICAL FEASIBILITY
Proven Tech Stack PostGIS, FastAPI, and React provide scalable, production-ready infrastructure
Config-Driven Scaling Platform uses adapters to onboard new state databases
Govt-Aligned Rollout Cloud-native architecture that supports DoLR’s "1 city + 1 village" phased expansion plan.
KEY CHALLENGES & RISKS
Data Inconsistency Different states use varying parcel IDs, formats, legacy schemas, and languages.
Legal Authority The system must not accidentally replace statutory records.
AI Bias & False Positives AI models might incorrectly flag legal records, causing administrative delays or unfair rejections
MITIGATION & VIABILITY
Adapters & ULPIN State-specific connectors translate diverse formats into a ULPIN-anchored common model.
Human-in-the-Loop The platform flags discrepancies for authorized officers to resolve.
Risk-Scoring Only AI only provides risk scores and decision-support to admins.

REFERENCE BASE FOR THE PROTOTYPE
– Department of Land Resources, MoRD - Digital India Land Records Modernization Programme (DILRMP) and the ULPIN "Bhu-Aadhaar" scheme
– NPCI Unified Payments Interface (UPI) - the Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) design paradigm the "Land UPI" model is adapted from
– India Stack - layered DPI framework (identity, payments, data-sharing) informing the "rail, not application" architecture
– Haryana Land Records Information System (HALRIS) - Jamabandi / Record-of-Rights, Khasra-Khatauni conventions used for the pilot village adapter
– OGC GeoJSON standard and the Shapely geometry library - geodesic area, centroid and polygon-subdivision logic in the spatial engine
– Academic literature on blockchain-based land registries - hash-chained, permissioned ledgers for tamper-evident title records
